Tabellar

Privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026.

Tabellar is open source software that runs on your own Mac, or on a Linux machine of your own if you set one up. There is no Tabellar server, no Tabellar account and no telemetry. Tabellar's developer never receives your mail, your calendar, your contacts, your sign-in tokens or any record of how you use the app.

What Tabellar accesses in your Google account

When you sign in with Google, Tabellar asks for these permissions and uses them for these purposes only:

  • Gmail (read, compose, send, label and archive): to copy your mail to your Mac, sort and summarise it, write reply drafts, send the mail you write or approve, add labels, archive what you ask it to, and unsubscribe from senders you choose. If you turn on Autopilot, it also sends replies on its own, only to the person who wrote to you, after a waiting period in which you can cancel. Tabellar has no function that deletes mail.
  • Google Calendar: to show your events, find free times and create the events you approve.
  • Contacts and your organisation's directory (read only): to suggest recipients and to recognise people you correspond with.
  • Your name, email address and profile picture: to show which account is connected.

Where your data is stored

On the machine you run Tabellar on only, called your Mac here. Mail, calendar entries, contacts and everything Tabellar derives from them (summaries, drafts, todos, the log of its actions) are kept in one database file in the .tabellar folder of your home folder. Google sign-in tokens, any AI provider key and the token of a Telegram bot are kept in the macOS Keychain, or, on a machine without one, in a file encrypted with a passphrase you choose. tabellar move writes all of it into one encrypted file for you to carry to another machine of yours. Tabellar listens only on your own machine and is not reachable from the network.

Who your data is shared with

Nobody, with two exceptions that you control: the AI model you choose, and a Telegram bot if you connect one.

  • A model on your Mac (through Ollama): the text of your mail never leaves your computer.
  • A cloud AI provider that you pick and connect with your own API key: the text of the mail being read, summarised or answered is sent from your Mac directly to that provider, under your own account and that provider's terms. It does not pass through Tabellar's developer. Tabellar tells you this before you choose, and you can switch to a model on your Mac at any time.
  • Telegram, off unless you make a bot of your own and paste its token: what needs you is sent from your Mac to that bot, in the one chat you paired. That is who wrote, the summary and the short answers you can give, a longer draft when you ask to read it, your events of the day, and the answers to what you ask there. Attachments are never sent. Telegram can read messages to and from bots. You can disconnect it in Settings at any time, and "Show sender and summary" turned off keeps it to saying that mail arrived.

Your data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used by Tabellar or its developer to train AI models. No person other than you reads it.

Tabellar's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your Mac until you remove it. Disconnecting a Google account in Settings revokes Tabellar's access to it. Deleting the .tabellar folder in your home folder removes the stored mail and settings, and the Keychain items named tabellar hold the tokens and keys. You can also remove Tabellar's access at any time from your Google account's permissions page.

This website

These pages are static files hosted by GitHub Pages. They set no cookies and run no analytics. GitHub, as the host, may log requests as described in its own privacy statement.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page, with their history in the project's repository. Questions go to the issue tracker.
