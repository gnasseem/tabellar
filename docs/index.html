What it never does

  • Delete your mailIt has no function for that. It can only take back drafts of its own.
  • Send before you allow itBelow Autopilot, nothing goes out without you.
  • Obey an emailMail that gives orders to an AI gets a label and no more.
  • Invent a number or a dateThose, addresses and links in a draft are checked against your mail.

Three ways to run it

Free

$0, all of it, open source

On your own computer, with a model that runs on your Mac or your own API key. Your mail stays in one file on your machine.

npm install -g tabellar
tabellar start

Paste the two lines into Terminal. The second opens Tabellar's window, which takes you through the rest. You need macOS and Node.js 24 or newer.

Your Google account

When you sign in with Google, Tabellar asks to read and manage your Gmail, your Google Calendar and your contacts. It uses them only on your own computer: to show and sort your mail, draft and send the replies you approve, add the labels and calendar events you ask for, and suggest recipients. None of it is sent to Tabellar's developer. The privacy policy says this in full.

Tabellar also works with iCloud, Fastmail, Yahoo, AOL, Zoho, GMX and any mailbox that takes an app password, and with Outlook once you have registered a free app with Microsoft. The README has the rest.
